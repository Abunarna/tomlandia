# Supplied sprite style study

The four supplied references are now local, checksummed inputs. They are small JPEG style guides rather than transparent production layers, so their useful evidence is visual language—not exact pixel copying or registration.

## Shared visual language

- Near-black, high-contrast outer contours keep silhouettes readable against the black presentation background.
- Forms are front-facing or near-front-facing, with feet separated and weight centred. The stance is compact rather than anatomically tall.
- Surfaces use a small number of stepped light, mid, and shadow values. Highlights describe material edges instead of airbrushed volume.
- Details cluster around the face, chest, belt, hands, and equipment. Quieter legs and outer shapes stop the sprite becoming uniformly noisy.
- Asymmetry comes from held equipment, cloth ends, belts, and ornaments while the underlying body remains stably registered.

## Reference roles

- **Dark crusader:** high-tier heavy-armour benchmark—layered plate, sharp shoulder silhouette, jewel accents, engraved edges, and ceremonial authority. It supports Shadowsteel, Voidsteel, Ancient, and Ascendant gravitas, but its shield and helmet are inspiration only.
- **Desert spearman:** low/mid light-armour benchmark—readable cloth wraps, leather bands, small metal accents, and restrained ornament. It demonstrates how Copper through Steel can feel complete without visual excess.
- **Adventurer:** base-character benchmark—large expressive head, warm skin, strong hair mass, compact torso, clear boots/gloves, and controlled accent colours. The production base must remain bald and lightly dressed so faces, grayscale hair, and armour can overlay it.
- **Glacial wyrm:** material benchmark—icy blue value steps, white edge highlights, repeating scales, crystalline horns, and increasing size toward focal features. These cues should inform Froststeel and Glacial without turning humanoid armour into a dragon costume.

## Generation implications

The first candidate should be a bald, lightly dressed male/female pair—not armour. It must prove proportions, foot baseline, head/hair envelope, neck seam, wrist/grip socket, and readability at game scale. After approval, generate one restrained Copper set and one visually ambitious Glacial or Shadowsteel set plus their large swords. Those four extremes test the progression and overlay boundaries before producing the entire catalogue.

Prompts should combine the fixed item keywords with shared constraints: `front-facing`, `compact fantasy sprite`, `stepped pixel shading`, `near-black outline`, `transparent 384x384 canvas`, and the named registration mask. Never ask an image generator to compose the whole character when producing a layer; request only the bald body, hair, armour, or split weapon layer against the locked registration template.
