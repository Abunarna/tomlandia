# Armour and sword generation keywords

Every generated item begins with the fixed two-word vocabulary in `AVATAR_TIER_ART_DIRECTION`. The material pair establishes colour and surface language; the heavy, light, or sword pair establishes silhouette. These are constraints, not complete image prompts.

Low tiers deliberately favour familiar construction and broad, readable forms. Ornament, asymmetry, supernatural light, silhouette width, and apparent power increase gradually. Heavy armour remains plate-led; light armour remains cloth, leather, mail, and fitted scale-led. Every sword stays a large two-handed/readable weapon within the split rear/front weapon masks.
